06 About the agency

Three towns, one small agency, no script.

Only as many clients as we can personally walk through a property with.

Chandra Estates started as two agents who were tired of pushy sales targets. We still take on only as many clients as we can personally walk through a property with — which means we turn work away most weeks.

  • We say no. If a property doesn't fit your list, we'll tell you before you book a viewing.
  • We know the streets. Drainage, noise, school catchments — the things a listing photo won't show.
  • We negotiate in writing. You see every offer and counter-offer as it happens.
